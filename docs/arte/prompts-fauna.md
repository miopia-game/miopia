## Guia de cor

```
Single clean professional video game color key / visual development sheet. Re-design the color costumes and graphic markings of these twelve ORIGINAL Miopia dark woodland creatures while preserving their exact identities and anatomy as seen in the references. The three supplied images show the current cast; reference 1 four forest enemies, reference 2 four undergrowth enemies, reference 3 four bosses. Make one compact meaningful pose per individual in a strict FOUR COLUMN x THREE ROW catalogue. Row 1 in order: round spider with ivory chalk SILK abdomen dark indigo legs and ember eye cluster; bat with rich hammered copper near-wing membrane and petrol-navy body with pale cheek, both wings spread; wasp with sulfur yellow bands, black-green shell and pale opal wings; ground beetle with hard malachite/turquoise shell and glimpses of cold pale jade stripe. Row 2: shadow leaf moth with inky blue body and ragged spectral periwinkle leaf flaps; woodland adder in vermilion lacquer with sage belly and small black diamonds; bark weevil with olive moss and warm ochre bark on a deep chocolate body; purple hooded snake with cream crescent marks. Row 3: torn-wing bat boss with crimson membranes and bone face; heavy spider boss with hanging moon-green silk, peat blue shell and pale sage moss; horned cracked boss beetle with obsidian indigo shell and molten amber fissures; owl with smoked blue-violet plumage, broad ivory facial disk and tawny primary feathers. Each reads unmistakably at thumbnail size: just three or four hard-edged masses per material, bold silhouette, restrained saturation with one clear accent. Crisp clean variable-thickness ink, dark plum-black outer edges, controlled gouache/cloisonne-like inner color blocks, intentional broad graphic motifs. Like a genuinely cohesive, authored, dark hand-inked 2D forest adventure. Absolutely no airbrush, faded texture, glow, cast shadows, gradient background, fuzzy lines, halos, text, captions, lettering or labels. Each full figure isolated and well separated in its own cell on a perfectly flat very dark slate blue background, each comfortably within its own area. Landscape sheet matching a polished art director's set of color keys for a 2D game. No environmental effects or weapons.
```
