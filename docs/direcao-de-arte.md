# Direção de arte de Miopia

Os oito estudos recebidos foram consultados para a silhueta dos insetos, os materiais e os mecanismos. A arte final abaixo foi gerada com ImageGen integrado ao Codex e recortada para uso offline. As colisões, a máscara de visão, os sinais e as animações são controlados pelo pygame. Cada folha tem canais de transparência reais. Os fundos opacos só ocupam o plano distante.

## Capa · assets/arte/capa.jpg

Use case: stylized-concept. Original key art for the title screen of Miopia, a melancholic Brazilian indie platform adventure. Wide 16:9 full bleed, absolutely no lettering. Dark traditional copperplate etching mixed with gouache and block print, rich fine hatch shading and dry brush ivory scratches. RIGHT two thirds: a tiny determined firefly in strict side profile with a segmented black chitin shell, imperfect folded translucent wings, cloudy eye and cracked warm amber lantern abdomen, on moss-covered arch of a great fig root. Small solitary insect hero faces left; amber lights the worn bark. Behind it: veils of silver teal mist, delicate leaves of Atlantic rainforest, twisting dark fig roots and a few suspended droplets. A distant break of pale blue-green moonlight in upper right. LEFT two fifths: low-contrast deep ink blue forest shadows with minimal detail; title/menu will be typeset in code. DESIGN: restrained printmaking palette of blue-black, charcoal, eucalyptus, vellum, honey. Rich tonal separation, solemn natural history illustration, no humans, no architectural ruins, no masks, no text, no frames, no UI or watermarks.

## Quatro fundos · assets/arte/fundo_0.jpg a fundo_3.jpg

Use case: stylized-concept. Production environment atlas of FOUR individual landscape backgrounds for a side-scrolling nocturnal indie game, perfectly regular 2x2 grid, each quadrant a wide landscape painting (whole sheet wide 16:9). NO margins, borders, text, sprites, UI or graphic panels. Each quadrant fills exactly one quarter. All are distant backgrounds only, no gameplay floors, no platforms or other manmade objects. Handmade late-1800s botanical copper engraving over watercolor, graphic silhouette masses, fine carved hatch. Four different color keys and totally different silhouettes: TOP LEFT damp Brazilian fern grove with curving huge trunks, suspended fern fronds and mist bands in eucalyptus and storm blue; TOP RIGHT river gorge with branching cascades and layered mangrovelike exposed roots in slate turquoise; BOTTOM LEFT dry Brazilian bamboo thicket ('taquaral') with angled tall canes and narrow leaves in smoky olive and faded ochre; BOTTOM RIGHT underground fig grotto with a cathedral canopy of twisted roots and earthen fissures above underground pools in plum slate and muted mineral violet. Distant luminous clearing in each; lightest areas midtone teal/beige, many silhouettes and layered atmosphere. No creatures. Each landscape horizon near 65% height. Uniform medium, contemplative, sophisticated graphic game art, no photorealism.
