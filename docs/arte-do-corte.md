# Arte do Corte da Figueira

O fundo distante foi produzido com ImageGen integrado ao Codex e exportado para `assets/arte/corte_da_figueira.jpg`. A copa, a casca, as galerias e o espelho d’água pertencem à mesma pintura. As superfícies jogáveis, os marcos, a escuridão, os objetos e o mapa ficam em camadas separadas, posicionadas pela câmera do mundo.

## Prompt de produção

Original vertical background painting for Miopia, a Brazilian Atlantic Forest 2D game. Layout: a single tall portrait, an immersive continuous side cutaway following the full height of one ancient strangler fig. TOP QUARTER: backlit boughs, distant blanket of tropical canopy, bromeliads and cool night mist, the moon behind a small fork. SECOND QUARTER: the thick textured trunk at mossy forest floor, massive living roots surrounding a hollow through which a thin waterfall disappears. THIRD QUARTER: belowground layers of clay, quartz and capillary fig roots, crossing galleries hinted in the distance, small pale amber fungi like stars. BOTTOM: blue-grey freshwater cistern deeply underground with root archways reflected in the still water. Hand-carved copperplate etching over gouache with inky hatch, subtle bone edge accents, teal gray foliage to subterranean mineral violet and modest amber. A world of insect scale, solemn and quietly inviting. True 2D side view; an atmospheric distant layer of a game, no playable foreground platform, invisible HUD, no doors/icons/text, no characters or logos, no diagram framing. Clearly distinct altitude bands, unified scene, crossed twigs, threadlike roots, wavy water reflection. Avoid a central floating island, no digital gloss or white background. Compose for a taller than wide canvas.
